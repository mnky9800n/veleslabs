Building an AI powered x-ray for the Earth’s subsurface.

Sparse data in, layers and traps out. The same model serves oil and gas, carbon capture and storage (CCS), and geological hydrogen.

Summary

  • Between wells, the rock is unmeasured, new acreage is sparsely measured, someone still has to decide what is there before a well is drilled.
  • We are building a “World Model” of the Earth’s crust that can be queried like a system, not like an exploration survey.
  • It will be trained on open data from the Norwegian DISKOS database, the best public basin-scale database in the world.
  • Norway is the market entry point, selling model licenses that will work for subsurface intelligence needs in CCS, geological hydrogen, and oil and gas.
  • Ships as a licence pack and an API / MCP endpoint into existing subsurface tools.

Market

  • 50% of oil remains undiscovered in Norway (Sodir, 2026)
  • 70 new offshore areas available for exploration in 2026 (Norwegian Ministry of Energy, 2026)
  • Expert Analytics offers market connections (e.g., Vår Energi meeting October) through their own clients and relationships with oil and gas industry in Norway
